01 · THE PROBLEM

HR usually finds out too late.

Reactive

Most companies learn someone is unhappy only when the resignation arrives. By then, nothing can be fixed.

Expensive

Replacing an employee is commonly estimated at half to double their annual salary once hiring, onboarding and lost output are counted.

Predictable

Overtime, satisfaction, pay, tenure and promotions leave a pattern in the data. A model can learn that pattern.
